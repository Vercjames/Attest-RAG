Opening an Account

What do I need to open an account?

You will need a government-issued photo ID, your Social Security number or ITIN, and a U.S. residential address.

Is there a minimum opening deposit?

  • Essential Checking: $25
  • Plus Checking: $100
  • Premier Checking: $1,000

How long does it take?

Most applications are approved within minutes. If we need more information, we will email you.
