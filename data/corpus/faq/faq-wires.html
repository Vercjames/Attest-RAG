Sending a Wire Transfer

How do I send a wire?

Sign in to online banking, choose Transfers, then Wire Transfer. You will need the recipient's name, bank routing number or SWIFT code, and account number.

When will my wire arrive?

Domestic wires submitted before 4:00 p.m. ET on a business day usually arrive the same day. International wires usually take 1 to 3 business days.

Can I cancel a wire?

Wires generally cannot be cancelled once sent. Contact us right away if you made a mistake.
