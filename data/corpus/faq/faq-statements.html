Statements and Documents

How do I get my statements?

Statements are available in online banking under Documents. You can choose e-statements or paper statements.

Is there a charge for paper statements?

A paper statement fee may apply depending on your account. See the Fee and Rate Schedule.

How far back can I see statements?

Online banking shows up to 7 years of statements.
