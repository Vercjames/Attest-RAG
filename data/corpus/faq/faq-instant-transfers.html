MB Instant Transfers

What is MB Instant Transfers?

A way to send money to friends and family using their email address or mobile number.

Is there a fee?

There is no fee to send or receive money with MB Instant Transfers.

Can I cancel a payment?

Only if the recipient has not enrolled yet. Payments to enrolled recipients are sent within minutes and cannot be cancelled.
