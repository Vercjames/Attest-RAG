Disputing a Charge

How do I dispute a charge?

Open the transaction in the mobile app and tap Dispute, or call Card Services.

How long does a dispute take?

Most disputes are resolved in about 10 days. Some can take longer.

Will I get my money back while you investigate?

In many cases we issue a temporary credit while we review your dispute.
