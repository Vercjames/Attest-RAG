Mobile Check Deposit

How much can I deposit with mobile deposit?

Mobile deposit limits are $5,000 per day and $20,000 per month for Essential and Plus Checking, and $10,000 per day and $40,000 per month for Premier Checking.

What is the cutoff time?

Deposits submitted before 9:00 p.m. ET on a business day are considered received that day.

What should I do with the paper check?

Write "For mobile deposit only at MerchantBridge" under your signature, and keep the check for 14 days before destroying it.
