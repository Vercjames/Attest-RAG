Overdraft FAQ

How much is an overdraft fee?

Our overdraft fee is $35 per item, with a maximum of 3 fees per day.

Can I avoid overdraft fees?

Yes. Set up low-balance alerts in the mobile app, or link a savings account for overdraft protection transfers.

How do I opt out of overdraft coverage?

You can opt out of overdraft coverage for debit card purchases and ATM withdrawals in online banking under Account Settings.
