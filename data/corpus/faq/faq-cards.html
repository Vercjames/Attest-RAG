Debit Card Help

I lost my card. What should I do?

Lock your card in the mobile app right away, then report it lost. We will send a replacement card.

Do I need to tell you before I travel?

No. You do not need to set a travel notice. Keep your mobile number up to date so we can reach you with fraud alerts.

How do I activate a new card?

Activate your card in the mobile app or by calling the number on the sticker.
