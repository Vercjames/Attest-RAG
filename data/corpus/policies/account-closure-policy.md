# Account Closure and Dormancy Policy

## Customer-initiated closure

Customers may close a deposit account at any time by visiting a branch or calling Customer Support. Any remaining balance is paid by check or transferred to another MerchantBridge account. An early closure fee applies to accounts closed within 90 days of opening; see the Fee and Rate Schedule.

## Pending items

We recommend customers wait until all pending transactions and checks have cleared before closing an account. Items presented after closure will be returned unpaid.

## Dormant accounts

An account with no customer-initiated activity for 12 months is classified as dormant. A dormant account fee may apply. Logging in to online banking does not count as activity; a deposit, withdrawal, or transfer does.

## Unclaimed property

If an account remains dormant, state law may require us to transfer the balance to the state as unclaimed property. We send a notice to the address on file before doing so.
