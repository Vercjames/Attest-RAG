# MB Instant Transfers Policy

## Overview

MB Instant Transfers lets customers send money to other people using an email address or U.S. mobile number. Transfers to enrolled recipients typically arrive within minutes.

## Limits

| Account tier | Daily limit | Monthly limit |
|---|---|---|
| Essential and Plus Checking | $2,500.00 | $10,000.00 |
| Premier Checking | $5,000.00 | $20,000.00 |
| Business Checking | Not available | Not available |

## Cancellations

Payments to enrolled recipients cannot be cancelled once sent. Payments to recipients who have not enrolled can be cancelled until the recipient enrolls.

## Fraud warning

Customers should only send money to people they know and trust. Instant transfers are generally not eligible for dispute if the customer authorized the payment.
