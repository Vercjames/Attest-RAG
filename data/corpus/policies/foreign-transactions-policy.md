# Foreign Transactions

## Foreign transaction fee

A foreign transaction fee applies to debit card purchases and ATM withdrawals made in a foreign currency, or processed outside the United States even if made in U.S. dollars. The fee is a percentage of the transaction amount; see the Fee and Rate Schedule.

## Exchange rates

Transactions in a foreign currency are converted to U.S. dollars using the rate set by the card network on the processing date, which may differ from the rate on the purchase date.

## Travel notices

Customers do not need to notify us before traveling. Our fraud monitoring takes travel patterns into account automatically.
