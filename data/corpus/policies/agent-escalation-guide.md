# Agent Escalation and Routing Guide

## Account-specific questions

Questions about a specific customer's balance, transactions, or account history must be answered from the authenticated agent console, not from the knowledge base. Verify the caller first, then open the account record.

## Products we do not service

Mortgage, auto loan, and investment questions are handled by MerchantBridge partner institutions. Transfer these callers to the Partner Products line. Contact center agents should not quote rates or terms for these products.

## Fraud and security

Any report of shared credentials, account takeover, or a scam involving money movement goes to Fraud Operations.

## Complaints

If a customer asks to file a formal complaint, log it in the complaint tracker before the end of the call.
