# 2026 Holiday Schedule

## Bank holidays

MerchantBridge observes Federal Reserve holidays. On these days, branches are closed and wires, ACH transfers, and check deposits are not processed: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day.

## Contact center hours

The contact center is open 24 hours a day for lost or stolen cards and fraud. All other support is available 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. ET, including holidays.
