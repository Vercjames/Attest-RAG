# Customer Identity Verification Policy

## Opening an account

To open an account, we collect the customer's name, date of birth, residential address, and taxpayer identification number. We verify identity using an unexpired government-issued photo ID.

## Verifying callers

Before discussing account-specific information, agents must verify the caller using the authentication steps in the agent console. Agents must never ask a customer for their full card PIN or online banking password.

## Additional documentation

We may request additional documentation if we cannot verify identity electronically. Accounts that cannot be verified within 30 days of opening may be closed.
