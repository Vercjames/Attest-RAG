# Overdraft Policy

## Purpose

This policy describes how MerchantBridge handles transactions that would overdraw a personal checking account.

## Overdraft fee

When we pay an item that overdraws your account, we charge an overdraft fee of $35.00 per item. We charge no more than 3 overdraft fees per business day.

We do not charge an overdraft fee if your account is overdrawn by $5.00 or less at the end of the business day.

## Declined items

If we decline to pay an item, we may charge a returned item fee. See the Fee and Rate Schedule for the current amount.

## Opting in

Overdraft coverage for one-time debit card purchases and ATM withdrawals requires your consent. You may opt in or out at any time through online banking or by calling Customer Support.
