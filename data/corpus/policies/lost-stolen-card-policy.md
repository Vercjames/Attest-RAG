# Lost or Stolen Card Policy

## Reporting

Customers should report a lost or stolen debit card immediately through the mobile app, online banking, or by calling Card Services 24 hours a day. When a card is reported lost or stolen, we block it permanently and it cannot be reactivated.

## Temporary lock

If a customer is unsure whether the card is lost, they can temporarily lock it in the mobile app and unlock it later.

## Replacement cards

Replacement cards ship by standard mail and arrive in 5 to 7 business days. Expedited delivery arrives in 2 business days. Fees for expedited delivery are listed in the Fee and Rate Schedule.

## Customers traveling outside the United States

If a card is lost while the customer is traveling outside the United States, we can send an expedited replacement card to an international address. International delivery typically takes 3 to 5 business days. Customers can continue to use digital wallet payments if their phone is not also lost, as the digital card number is reissued automatically.

## Liability

Customers are not responsible for unauthorized transactions reported promptly. See the Debit Card Dispute Policy for dispute timelines.
