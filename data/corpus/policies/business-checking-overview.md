# Business Checking Overview

## Who it's for

Business Checking is available to sole proprietors, partnerships, LLCs, and corporations with a U.S. taxpayer identification number.

## Fees

Business Checking has its own column in the Fee and Rate Schedule. Business fees for wires, overdrafts, and returned items differ from personal checking fees.

## Features not available

MB Instant Transfers is not available on Business Checking. Business customers can use ACH origination through Business Online Banking instead.

## Authorized signers

Business accounts may have up to 8 authorized signers. Adding or removing a signer requires a signed resolution from the business.
