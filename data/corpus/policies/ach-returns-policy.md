# ACH Returns and Returned Items

## Outgoing items we return unpaid

If an ACH debit or check is presented against your account and your available balance is not sufficient, we may return the item unpaid. This is commonly called a returned item or an NSF (non-sufficient funds) return. A returned item fee applies per item; see the Fee and Rate Schedule.

We do not charge a returned item fee for an item that is re-presented by the same merchant within 30 days after we returned it.

## Deposited items that are returned to us

If a check or ACH credit you deposited is returned unpaid by the paying bank, we will reverse the credit from your account. A deposited item returned fee may apply.

## Return reason codes

Common reason codes include R01 (insufficient funds), R02 (account closed), and R03 (no account or unable to locate account). Agents can view the reason code on the transaction detail screen.
