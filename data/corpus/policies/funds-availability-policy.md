# Funds Availability Policy

## General policy

Funds from check deposits are generally available as follows: the first $225.00 is available on the first business day after the day of deposit, and the remainder is available on the second business day after the day of deposit.

## Cash and electronic deposits

Cash deposited at a branch and incoming electronic direct deposits are available on the day we receive them.

## Business days and cutoffs

A business day is Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays. Deposits made at an ATM before 8:00 p.m. ET, or through mobile deposit before 9:00 p.m. ET, are considered received that business day.

## Longer delays

We may hold funds longer for new accounts during the first 30 days, for deposits over $5,525.00 on any one day, for redeposited checks, or when we have reasonable cause to doubt collectibility. If we place a longer hold, we will tell the customer when the funds will be available, which is generally no later than the seventh business day after the day of deposit.
