# Wire Transfer Policy

## Domestic wires

Outgoing domestic wire requests received by 4:00 p.m. ET on a business day are processed the same business day. Requests received after the cutoff are processed the next business day.

## International wires

Outgoing international wire requests must be received by 2:00 p.m. ET on a business day. International wires typically arrive in 1 to 3 business days, depending on the destination country and intermediary banks. Intermediary banks may deduct their own fees from the amount received.

## Fees

Wire fees depend on direction (incoming or outgoing), destination (domestic or international), and account tier. See the Fee and Rate Schedule.

## Recalls

Once a wire has been sent, it generally cannot be cancelled. We can request a recall, but the receiving bank is not obligated to return the funds. A recall request does not guarantee recovery.

## Verification

Outgoing wires of $10,000.00 or more require a verbal callback to the phone number on file before release.
