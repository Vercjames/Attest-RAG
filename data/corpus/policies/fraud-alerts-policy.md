# Fraud Alerts and Suspicious Activity

## Fraud alerts

When we detect a suspicious debit card transaction, we send a text alert asking the customer to confirm whether they made it. Customers reply YES or NO. A NO reply blocks the card and opens a fraud case.

## What we never ask

MerchantBridge will never ask a customer to share a one-time passcode, PIN, or password by phone, text, or email, and will never ask a customer to move money to a "safe account."

## Escalation

If a customer reports they shared credentials or moved money at someone's request, agents should transfer the call to Fraud Operations immediately rather than attempting to resolve it.
