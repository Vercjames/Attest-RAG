# Debit Card Dispute Policy

## Filing a dispute

You must notify us of an unauthorized or incorrect debit card transaction within 60 days after we send the first statement on which the transaction appears. Disputes can be filed in online banking, in the mobile app, or by calling Card Services.

## Provisional credit

If we need more than 10 business days to complete our investigation, we will provisionally credit your account for the disputed amount within 10 business days of receiving your notice.

## Investigation timeline

We complete most investigations within 45 days. For transactions initiated outside the United States, point-of-sale transactions, and transactions on accounts open less than 30 days, the investigation may take up to 90 days.

## Outcome

We will notify you of the result within 3 business days of completing the investigation. If we determine no error occurred, we will reverse any provisional credit and notify you of the date and amount of the reversal.
