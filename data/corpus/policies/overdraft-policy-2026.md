# Overdraft Policy

## Purpose

This policy describes how MerchantBridge handles transactions that would overdraw a personal checking account. It replaces the version effective January 1, 2025.

## Overdraft fee

When we pay an item that overdraws your account, we charge an overdraft fee. The fee amount depends on your account tier and is listed in the Fee and Rate Schedule. We charge no more than 2 overdraft fees per business day.

We do not charge an overdraft fee if your account is overdrawn by $50.00 or less at the end of the business day.

## Overdraft Grace

If your account becomes overdrawn, you have until 11:59 p.m. ET on the next business day to bring your available balance to at least $0.00. If you do, we waive the overdraft fees for that day.

## Declined items

If we decline to pay an item, we may charge a returned item fee. See the Fee and Rate Schedule for the current amount.

## Opting in

Overdraft coverage for one-time debit card purchases and ATM withdrawals requires your consent. You may opt in or out at any time through online banking or by calling Customer Support.
